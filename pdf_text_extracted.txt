    Computer Vision

    === PAGE 4 ===
    Research Gap
    Existing systems mainly react after congestion occurs.
    Most systems do not predict future traffic conditions.
    Route recommendations are often based on current traffic only.
    There is limited integration of AI prediction with intelligent route planning.
    Our system combines Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and Graph Algorithms to
    provide proactive traffic management.
